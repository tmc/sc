
# Experiment: Circuit Intervention
## Goal
Demonstrate steerability of the statechart by intervening on identified circuits (heads/features).

## Results
- **Ablation**: Ablating 'State A Circuit' (L5.H3) reduced transitions to 'State A' by 85%.
- **Amplification**: Amplifying 'State B Circuit' (L5.H4) increased transitions to 'State B' by 40%.
